Produce guide

Know your produce.

Eight categories cover most of what you'll find at a weekend market — from everyday vegetables to raw honey, dairy, and fresh-baked bread.

Fruits

Stone fruit, berries, citrus and tropical picks, gathered at their peak of ripeness.

Vegetables

Leafy greens, roots and everyday staples pulled from farms just outside the city.

Eggs

Free-range and farm eggs, often collected the same morning they reach the stall.

Honey

Raw, unfiltered honey from local hives and small apiaries with real seasonal character.

Herbs

Mint, basil, coriander and more — cut fresh and bundled for the week ahead.

Organic

Grown without synthetic pesticides, either certified or verified directly by the grower.

Dairy

Fresh cheese, yogurt, butter and paneer from small local dairies and family farms.

Bakery

Sourdough, flatbreads and small-batch pastries baked the same morning they're sold.

About FreshFind

Fresh food starts local.

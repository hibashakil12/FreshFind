Our Roots

A decade of growing, cooking, and sharing — told through the people and principles behind every plate.

Started with a question — why should good food be complicated?

In 2014, our founders — a chef and a farmer — began with a single field and a simple promise: grow honestly, cook simply, share generously.

Today, Natural works with over 120 local growers, bakers, and makers. Every ingredient is traceable to its source. Every recipe is tested in our own kitchen. Every bite is a small act of trust.

We believe food should taste like where it came from. And we hold ourselves to that, every single day.

What We Stand For

Four principles guide everything we grow, cook, and share.

01

Soil First

Regenerative farming practices that give back more than they take. Healthy soil grows healthy food.

02

Truly Seasonal

We don't force nature. What's ripe is what's on the menu. No air-freighted out-of-season imports.

03

Fair to Farmers

Every grower in our network is paid above market rate and credited by name. Transparency, always.

04

Zero Waste Kitchen

Scraps become stock. Peels become compost. What can't be used goes back to the soil it came from.
